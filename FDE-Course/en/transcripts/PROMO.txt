Forward Deployed Delivery helps technical professionals practice the transition to end-to-end delivery ownership. Follow one fictional support engagement through discovery, framing, a working review prototype, evidence-based decisions, adoption, and reuse. Then transfer the method to a maintenance capstone. The course includes thirty-six lessons, a guided coding lab, reusable templates, worked answers, captions, and a complete Spanish edition. Some coding is required. No paid AI account is needed for the supplied rule-based lab. You will build a simulated deployment portfolio and a plan for gathering real field evidence. Course completion describes educational achievement, without accredited certification or guaranteed career outcomes. Narration uses synthetic voices. Begin with the preview lesson to see the learning approach.
