# Deployment toolkit
Use these editable templates alongside your portfolio. Empty fields are intentional learner inputs. Record the evidence source and date. Keep prior versions.

## 1. Site Map v0 / v1
- Engagement and version:
- Actual decision and authorized decider:
- People: users, owner, approver, maintainer, blockers, consequence bearers:
- Process: before, during, after; handoffs and exceptions:
- Technology: applications, interfaces, dependencies:
- Data: origin, freshness, quality, permitted use:
- Constraints: tag physical / political / habitual; identify authority:
- Incentives: benefits, resistance, accountability:
- History: earlier attempts and observed failure reasons:
- Verification status: claim / known-assumed-unknown / source / date / next test / owner:

## 2. Say/do gap log
Record: reported behavior / observed behavior / scope of observation / competing explanation / next observation. A discrepancy invites investigation, not an accusation.

## 3. Frame Card
For [user], improve [workflow] from [current condition] to [desired condition], measured by [metric with denominator], within [constraints].
If we believe [hypothesis], we should do [smallest action]. We will change our mind if [evidence].
- Operator agreement, source and date:
- Baseline definition and eligible population:
- Quality guardrails and stop conditions:
- Exclusions and unresolved dependencies:
- Gate decision owner and review date:

## 4. Prediction log
Before action, record: timestamp / hypothesis / expected result / measure and denominator / scope / confidence with reason / evidence that would change the decision / planned reading date. Preserve the original entry.

## 5. Delta log and gate
Record: prediction reference / observed result / denominator and exclusions / difference / alternative explanations / updated belief / land-iterate-pivot-stop decision / owner / next test. Use a separate entry for the second reading after adoption.

## 6. Landing checklist and adoption plan
For reliability, observability, security, integration, usability and adoption, record: evidence / readiness state / unresolved gap / owner / next action.
- Named operating owner, authority and backup:
- Manual fallback and disable trigger:
- Recovery test and escalation:
- Eligible work denominator and adoption period:
- Outcome reading date:

## 7. Distill pack
- Pattern: principle, conditions, mechanism, limits:
- Primitive: interface, version, tests, maintainer, local configuration:
- Playbook: repeatable method and improvement:
- Product signal: observation, consequence, scope, hypothesis, validation:
- Site capability: demonstrated handover behavior and feedback loop:

## 8. One-page decision memo
Decision required. Recommendation. Evidence and its scope. Known facts. Assumptions and unknowns. Options considered. Trade-offs. Authority boundary. Main risk, trigger and control. Next action, owner, date and reassessment condition.

## Worked starting set: Northstar
Decision: test reviewable category assistance using synthetic tickets. Maya owns the workflow, Priya approves data, Luis maintains integration. Known in the case: the prior automation increased checking work. Assumed: category lookup creates avoidable effort. Unknown: whether integrated suggestions will sustain use.
Frame: median review time of 20 minutes to at most 14, on twelve comparable simulated tasks, with human approval and at most one misroute. These are fictional exercise thresholds.
Prediction: assistance will meet those thresholds. Write your timestamp before opening the results.
First reading: median 12, one error, twelve eligible tasks, two excluded inputs. Prepare a controlled landing after checklist gaps close.
Second reading: 40 assisted tasks of 100 eligible, below the pre-agreed 70 percent adoption target. Iterate on duplicate-entry friction and incentives. Do not declare the full outcome achieved.
Distill: integration and trusted override handling matter alongside suggestion quality. Reuse the tested decision-recording contract. Keep the category vocabulary local.
